About

I’m a Senior Researcher in autonomous robotics focused on LiDAR–visual–inertial localization, state estimation, and robust multi-sensor localization. My recent work combines LIO-SAM / SC-LIO-SAM, GTSAM/iSAM2, calibrated LiDAR intensity, and visual/depth support for localization on real AMR and autonomous-forklift platforms.

Selected current work

Intensity-Assisted Localization

Calibrated LiDAR-intensity priormaps, Intensity Scan Context hypothesis prescoring, and small-GICP geometric validation for repetitive warehouse environments.

Visual-Aided LIO

Synchronized RGB-D / depth-supported visual feature projection and keyframe persistence while keeping the LiDAR–inertial pose backbone authoritative.

Real-Robot Localization

ESKF-based LiDAR–IMU localization, calibration, navigation integration, and quantitative validation on autonomous forklifts.

Technical focus

  • Localization / SLAM: LIO-SAM, SC-LIO-SAM, LVI-SAM evaluation / LIO–VIO integration, relocalization, GICP, Scan Context.
  • State estimation / optimization: GTSAM/iSAM2, IMU preintegration, ESKF/EKF/Kalman filtering, nonlinear least-squares, LOAM-style scan-to-map optimization and degeneracy handling.
  • Visual / intensity support: RGB-D visual feature integration, depth-supported 3D features, Intensity Scan Context, and calibrated LiDAR-intensity priormaps.
  • Calibration / sensor integration: inertial-sensor calibration, monocular camera calibration, PnP/RANSAC 2D–3D pose estimation, planar LiDAR/base SE(2), synchronization, and TF integration.

Research interests

  • Robust localization: global localization and relocalization in repetitive or geometrically ambiguous environments.
  • Multi-sensor state estimation: combining geometric, visual, inertial, and calibrated-intensity cues with explicit failure handling.
  • Calibration & observability: spatial/temporal sensor alignment and quantitative validation of calibration quality.
  • Navigation under uncertainty: stochastic estimation and control for reliable autonomous systems on real hardware.
